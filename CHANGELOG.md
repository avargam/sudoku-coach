## [0.5.0]

First public release.

- Point the camera at a paper sudoku: the app finds the board, outlines it live, reads several frames while you hold still, votes on each cell and opens the review on its own, usually in under 2 seconds. A manual shutter, a torch and a photo picker are there as fallbacks.
- Review the board before getting a hint. Cells the app is unsure about are outlined, and you can fix any digit with the keypad. The app checks for repeated digits, mistakes against the solution and whether the puzzle has a unique solution.
- Get the next logical step, not the solution: first the technique, then where to look, then what to place or eliminate, with an explanation. Thirteen human techniques, from singles to subsets, that match HoDoKu step by step.
- Everything runs on the phone, with no internet connection or account. Light and dark themes. The app is in Spanish.
